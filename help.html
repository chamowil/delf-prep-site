Help Center / FAQ

How is a mock exam scored?

Each mock exam follows the official DELF/DALF format: four sections (listening, reading, writing, speaking), each scored out of 25 points. To pass, you need at least 50/100 overall and at least 5/25 in every section — the same rule used in the real exam.

How does vocabulary review work?

Vocabulary flashcards use spaced repetition: words you mark as hard come back for review sooner, and words you know well are shown less often.

Can I change the app's instruction language?

Yes — Settings → Language lets you show instructions and explanations in any of 13 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Greek, Arabic, Hindi, Malagasy, Japanese, Chinese and Korean. Exam content itself always stays in French.

How does listening practice audio work?

Exercises play recorded French audio where available. You can adjust playback speed in Settings, and download an Enhanced or Premium system voice (Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French) for any text without a recording.

Can I reset my progress?

Yes — Settings → Progress → Reset all progress clears your results. Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all my data erases everything: progress, recordings and settings on this device, and the synced copy in your iCloud. There is no account to close.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your progress lives on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, syncs through your own private iCloud account to your other devices. Settings → Backup & Restore also lets you export a copy as a file. Speaking recordings stay on the device where they were made.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

On a new device, open the Premium screen or Settings → Premium and tap Restore purchases. Premium is billed by Apple: to cancel, use Settings → Premium → Manage subscription, or your Apple ID's Subscriptions page in the Settings app.
