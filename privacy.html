French it Up

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Short version: French it Up does not collect, track, or sell your personal data. The developer never receives your practice history, writing, or recordings. If you use iCloud, your progress syncs through your own private iCloud account, which the developer cannot read.

What we collect

Nothing. French it Up has no analytics SDK, no advertising network, no tracking, and no account. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

What's stored, and where

Your practice results, vocabulary progress, exam scores, streaks, achievements, writing drafts and answers, and words you add to your notebook are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is also kept in sync across your devices through the private iCloud database of your own Apple ID. Apple stores it on your behalf under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it.

You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Delete all my data. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking-practice recordings and their transcriptions are used only so you can play back and review your own answers. Speech recognition runs on your device. Recordings stay on the device where they were made — they are not uploaded, shared, or synced.

Camera

If you use the camera vocabulary feature, the camera image is analysed on your device to suggest the French word for an object. Photos are never saved or uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app only learns whether you have an active subscription; it never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device — no data about your usage is sent to us or anyone else to generate them.

Children's privacy

Because French it Up collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app's content is educational French-language exam practice material suitable for learners of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
