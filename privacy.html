DELF Prep

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

Short version: DELF Prep does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything you do in the app — practice history, XP, streaks, recordings — stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing is sent off your device. DELF Prep has no analytics SDK, no advertising network, and no backend server. There is no account required to use the app.

What's stored, and where

Your practice results, vocabulary progress, exam scores, streaks, and achievements are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is never transmitted anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account data.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking-practice recordings and their on-device transcriptions are used only to let you play back and review your own answers. Audio is processed using on-device speech recognition and is never uploaded or shared.

Notifications

If you enable streak reminders, the reminder is scheduled locally through iOS — no data about your usage is sent to us or anyone else to generate it.

Advertising

DELF Prep does not currently show ads or use any third-party ad SDK. Settings includes an ad-tracking preference for forward compatibility; it has no effect today. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and honor your preference.

Children's privacy

Because DELF Prep collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app's content is educational French-language exam practice material suitable for learners of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
